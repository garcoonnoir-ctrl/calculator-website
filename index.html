How Our Calculators Work

QuickCalc provides simple online tools for common mathematical and financial calculations. Each calculator is designed to give you a quick result without requiring any additional software.

Percentage Calculator

Use the percentage calculator to find a percentage of a number. For example, you can calculate 20% of 150 quickly and easily.

Percentage Change Calculator

The percentage change calculator shows how much a value has increased or decreased compared with its original value. It is useful for comparing prices, measurements, statistics and other values.

Discount Calculator

The discount calculator helps you find how much money you save after applying a percentage discount and shows the final price.

Average Calculator

Enter several numbers separated by commas to calculate their average quickly.

Profit and Loss Calculator

Compare your cost price and selling price to determine whether a transaction results in a profit, a loss or neither.

Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate simple interest using the principal amount, interest rate and time in years.

Compound Interest Calculator

Use the compound interest calculator to estimate how an investment or savings balance can grow when interest is added to the principal over time. You can choose how often the interest is compounded.

Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth to calculate the person's current age in complete years.

Basic Calculator

Perform common arithmetic operations including addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
